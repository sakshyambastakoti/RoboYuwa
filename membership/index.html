Why Join

What You Gain as a Member

RoboYuwa provides end-to-end support for aspiring robotics engineers, researchers, and tech pioneers across Nepal.

Hands-On Hardware Labs

Get direct access to PCB fabrication, microcontroller labs, 3D printing, and our comprehensive electronics component library.

Competition Pathways

Subsidized entry to WRO, RoboCup, national hackathons, and RADAR — RoboYuwa's flagship annual innovation summit.

Expert Mentorship

Get paired 1-on-1 with industry-experienced mentors across robotics engineering, embedded systems, and software.

National Network

Connect with 120+ innovators across Nepal's 7 provinces through regional chapter workshops, hack nights, and Discord forums.

Certified Recognition

Earn official RoboYuwa credential certificates, project recommendation letters, and verifiable open hardware contribution credits.

Global Exposure

Represent Nepal in international robotics championships, world STEM forums, and collaborative global research partnerships.
