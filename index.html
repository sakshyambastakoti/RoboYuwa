About Us

A Leading & Pioneer Youth Robotics & Innovation Hub in Nepal.

Founded in June 2024, RoboYuwa is Nepal's premier youth-driven non-governmental organization dedicated to democratizing robotics, embedded systems, and applied research. We equip aspiring innovators with state-of-the-art lab access, hands-on hardware prototyping, and national-to-global championship mentorship to build homegrown technological solutions.

Hands-on Labs

Direct access to 3D printers, sensors, microcontrollers, and PCB prototyping tools.

Global Mentorship

Preparing competitive youth teams to innovate and excel in national and world championships.

Key Objectives

Our 4 Core Objectives to Empower Nepal's Next Generation.

At RoboYuwa, we don't just teach robotics — we ignite technological leadership. Every initiative we launch is driven by ethical engineering, hands-on fabrication, and sustainable community impact across Nepal.

01

Hands-On STEM Innovation

Promote experiential learning, creative problem-solving, and rapid hardware prototyping in robotics and emerging technologies.

02

Leadership & Teamwork

Develop leadership, analytical thinking, and youth mentorship through grassroots workshops, bootcamps, and real-world projects.

03

Ethical Tech Collaboration

Unite young innovators across Nepal for scientific collaboration, fostering an open-source, ethical, and inclusive culture.

04

Global Arena Competitions

Build combat, soccer, and autonomous robotics to actively represent Nepal on national and international championship stages.
