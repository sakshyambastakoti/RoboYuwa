Why Join

What You Gain as a Member

Hands-On Hardware Labs
Get direct access to PCB fabrication, microcontroller labs, 3D printing, and our full electronics component library.
Competition Pathways
Subsidized entry to WRO, RoboCup, national hackathons, and RADAR — RoboYuwa's flagship annual innovation event.
Expert Mentorship
Get paired with industry-experienced mentors from robotics engineering, embedded systems, and software development.
National Network
Connect with 120+ innovators across Nepal's 7 provinces through regional chapter meetups and Discord communities.
Certified Recognition
Earn official RoboYuwa participation certificates, participation letters, and open-source project contribution credits.
Global Exposure
Represent Nepal in international robotics competitions, world championships, and collaborative research exchanges.
