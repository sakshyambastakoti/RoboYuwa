RoboLearn Architecture

The 12-Week Learning Progression

A rigorous, project-based engineering pathway designed to transform complete novices into autonomous robotics creators capable of competing on national stages.

Phase 01 Weeks 1 – 3

Circuit Physics & Logic

Fundamental electronics, understanding voltage, current, Ohm's law, breadboard prototyping, LED arrays, and essential workshop safety.

  • Resistor color codes & Ohm's law
  • Breadboard circuit wiring
  • Transistors & relay switching
Capstone: Light-Activated Automatic Circuit
Phase 02 Weeks 4 – 6

Microcontrollers & C++

Transitioning into programmable hardware with Arduino Uno and C++. Learning GPIO registers, serial telemetry, analog inputs, and PWM motor drivers.

  • Arduino IDE setup & C++ syntax
  • H-Bridge L298N motor drivers
  • Serial monitoring & debugging
Capstone: Dual-Motor Wheeled Chassis
Phase 03 Weeks 7 – 9

Sensors & PID Algorithms

Integrating environmental perception using ultrasonic sonar, infrared arrays, and implementing closed-loop PID control loops for high-speed tracking.

  • 5-Array IR line tracking sensor
  • Ultrasonic obstacle avoidance
  • PID steering correction logic
Capstone: Autonomous Line Follower
Phase 04 Weeks 10 – 12

Arena Combat & Showcase

Mechanical structural reinforcement, wireless Bluetooth/RF manual override, arena battle tactics, and final tournament judging before external mentors.

  • Chassis torque & wheel traction
  • Bluetooth mobile remote control
  • Live arena combat tournament
Capstone: Tournament-Ready Combat Robot

Curriculum Details

RoboLearn 12-Week Syllabus Outline

Designed for schools and youth clubs across Nepal. All necessary hardware and components provided by RoboYuwa.

Week Topic & Domain Hardware & Tools Milestone Deliverable
W01 Electronics & Circuit Physics Breadboard, LEDs, 330Ω/10kΩ Resistors Series & parallel circuit testing
W02 Sensors & Analog Triggers LDR (Light sensor), POT, Transistors Automatic night-lamp circuit
W03 Relays & Actuators 5V Relays, DC Motors, Diodes High-current motor switching
W04 Microcontrollers & Arduino Arduino Uno, USB Telemetry, IDE Digital output & blink pattern logic
W05 Motor Drivers & H-Bridges L298N Driver, 12V Battery Pack Forward, reverse, and pivot steering
W06 PWM Speed Modulation Arduino PWM pins, optical encoders Smooth acceleration & brake tuning
W07 Infrared Arrays & Line Tracking TCRT5000 IR Sensor Array Black-line following calibration
W08 Ultrasonic Radar & Obstacles HC-SR04 Sonar, Servo Mount Real-time distance threshold braking
W09 Closed-Loop PID Control PID Algorithm tuning in C++ High-speed wobble-free navigation
W10 Chassis CAD & Structural Armor Acrylic chassis, zip-ties, spacers Rigid combat-proof assembly
W11 Bluetooth Remote Overrides HC-05 Module, Smartphone App Dual mode: Autonomous & Manual
W12 Grand Arena Championship RoboYuwa Competition Arena Live tournament, judging & awards
