Executive Summary & Tracking Transparency Pledge

RoboYuwa respects your digital sovereignty. **We do not deploy intrusive cross-site advertising pixels or sell browsing habits to data brokers.** The cookies utilized on our site serve fundamental security operations, remember essential user preferences (such as dark/light reading modes), and provide aggregated, anonymous metrics to help us expand robotics education across Nepal.

01

What Are Cookies & Tracking Technologies?

Foundational concepts and technical mechanics

Cookies are small text files placed onto your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you access web pages. They enable web applications to recognize your browser, persist user sessions between page navigations, and remember individual preferences.

In addition to standard HTTP cookies, RoboYuwa may utilize complementary modern browser technologies such as localStorage and sessionStorage to store non-sensitive state data (such as quick search history or visual modal dismissal flags) locally on your device without transmitting it across external networks.

02

Categories of Cookies We Utilize

Distinct operational roles and necessity levels

RoboYuwa categorizes all cookies into three clear functional groups:

1. Strictly Necessary

Crucial for security, CSRF protection, and application form submission. The site cannot function safely without these cookies.

2. Analytics & Performance

Collect aggregate, anonymized traffic counts to identify high-interest workshops and optimize server loading speeds.

3. Functional & Preferences

Remember user interface preferences, such as high-contrast settings or newsletter popup dismissals, for convenience.

04

Third-Party Integrations & Embedded Media

External services delivering enriched multimedia

To showcase student robotics prototypes and facilitate direct community correspondence, our website utilizes trusted external dependencies:

  • YouTube Embedded Demonstrations: We embed robotics match recordings and tutorial walkthroughs utilizing YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which limits tracking cookies until playback is initiated.
  • Google Fonts & CDNs: Typography assets and iconography are fetched via high-speed global delivery networks to guarantee minimal latency across all provinces of Nepal.
  • EmailJS Dispatch Infrastructure: Contact inquiries and membership submissions are processed using secure client-side tokenized APIs without storing persistent tracking cookies.
05

How to Control & Disable Cookies

Configuring browser privacy shields and preferences

You retain full authority to inspect, block, or delete cookies at any time through your browser's native privacy controls:

Google Chrome

Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies → Block third-party cookies.

Mozilla Firefox

Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection → Strict / Custom.

Apple Safari

Preferences → Privacy → Prevent cross-site tracking → Block all cookies.

Microsoft Edge

Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data.

Impact of Disabling Cookies: If you elect to reject strictly necessary session cookies, critical interactive capabilities—including the real-time membership application form and donor confirmation receipts—may fail to function correctly.
