WHO WE ARE & OUR STORY

Empowering Youth Through Robotics, Research & Ethical Innovation

Founded in June 2024, RoboYuwa is a youth-driven non-governmental organization entirely dedicated to robotics, technology, research, and hands-on innovation across Nepal.

Our Mission

To empower youth with technology skills, creative problem-solving, and leadership to transform communities.

Our Vision

A society where ethical technology and innovation are driven by capable, confident, and united youth creating sustainable community impact.
