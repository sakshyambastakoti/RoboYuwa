Cross-Functional Matrix

Interdepartmental Synergies & Workflows

As established in the RoboYuwa Constitution and Departmental Charter, no team operates in isolation. Specialized cross-departmental synergy pods ensure seamless execution from hardware labs to national arenas.

Tech & Outreach Pod

School Workshops & Lab Rollouts

Research & Technical prototypes hands-on modular kits, while Outreach coordinates with municipal schools and community youth hubs across all 7 provinces.

Event & Tech Pod

Combat Arenas & Hackathons

Event Operations synchronizes venue timelines, crowd safety, and judging panels, while R&D provisions the combat ring, electronic telemetry, and obstacle timing sensors.

Legal & Outreach Pod

Institutional MoUs & Sponsorships

Outreach leads corporate and educational liaisons, while Legal & Compliance drafts non-profit agreements, IP ownership policies, and statutory compliance files.

Creative & Event Pod

Immersive Stagecraft & Media

Creative & Media produces high-definition visual keynotes, arena branding, and 4K livestream broadcasts, giving national exposure to youth engineering talent.

Executive & Governance

Constitutional Oversight & Audits

Strict 51% Executive Board majority vote required for financial disbursements over NPR 5,000, validated by independent chartered auditors and legal advisors.

Tech & Media Pod

Interactive Portals & Simulators

Technical engineering collaborates directly with visual design to publish open-source schematics, web simulators, and interactive hardware tutorials for youth nationwide.
